How it works

Form your LLC in 4 steps

Everything happens online, with a contact person on WhatsApp at every step.

  1. 01

    10-minute form

    Company name, business activity, owners and ID document.

    You suggest up to three names: we check availability with the Wyoming registry.

  2. 02

    State filing

    We file your Articles of Organization in Wyoming and prepare your operating agreement.

    Your registered agent (local legal representative) is included for the first year.

  3. 03

    EIN

    We obtain your US tax ID from the IRS, without a Social Security number.

    The EIN is required to open a bank account and receive payments.

  4. 04

    Bank and Stripe

    We help you open an online account (Mercury, Wise Business…) and activate Stripe and PayPal.

    Included in the Ultimate and Ultimate Launch packages. Final approval depends on each bank or platform.

Documents you will need

  • A valid passport (preferred) or national ID card
  • Your full home address
  • Email and WhatsApp number
  • A description of your business
  • Name and share of each owner, if there are several

Timelines

The company is usually registered in Wyoming within a few business days. For non-residents, the EIN can take from a few days to several weeks depending on the IRS. We keep you updated on WhatsApp at every step.
