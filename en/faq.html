FAQ

Everything you want to know

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

Do I need to live in the US or have a visa?

No. An LLC can be formed by someone who does not live in the United States, with no visa and no travel. Everything is done online.

How long does it take?

The company is usually registered within a few business days. Getting the EIN from the IRS can take from a few days to several weeks for non-residents.

Why Wyoming?

It is a simple, low-cost state to maintain a small LLC, with no state income tax and a very light annual report.

Can I use Stripe from Morocco?

A US LLC with an EIN and a US bank account lets you apply for a US Stripe account. Final approval depends on Stripe and on your business.

What about Shopify Payments?

Shopify Payments requires a personal US number (SSN or ITIN), even with an LLC and an EIN. That is why we set up your store with Stripe and PayPal. If you want to try Shopify Payments, we can help you apply for an ITIN (paid option, approval not guaranteed).

Will I pay less tax?

Not automatically. Depending on your country of residence (France, Morocco…), you may remain taxable at home and have reporting obligations, including currency-exchange rules. We recommend consulting a tax adviser; we can put you in touch.

Which documents do I need?

A valid passport (or ID card), your full address, a description of your business and, if there are several owners, the name and share of each.

What is annual compliance?

Every year an LLC must keep a registered agent, pay its state annual report and file certain federal returns (including Form 5472 for foreign owners). Missing them can lead to heavy penalties: we handle it for you.
