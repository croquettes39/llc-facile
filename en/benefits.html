Benefits

Why a US LLC?

The structure international entrepreneurs prefer to sell online worldwide.

Access to US Stripe and PayPal

Take card payments from customers worldwide, even if Stripe is not available in your country.

US dollar bank account

Open a US business account online (Mercury, Wise Business…) to receive USD and EUR.

Sell anywhere

Digital products, courses, subscriptions or e-commerce: one company to sell in Europe, the US and beyond.

Limited liability

Your personal assets are separate from the company's: the LLC alone is liable for its debts.

No minimum capital

No capital to deposit to get started, and low annual fees in states like Wyoming.

100% remote

No visa, no travel, no need to live in the US. Everything is handled from home.

International credibility

A US company reassures customers, partners and platforms such as Amazon US and marketplaces.

Simple to run

Few formalities: a registered agent, an annual report and a few filings, which we can handle for you.

What is an LLC?

An LLC (Limited Liability Company) is a US company with limited liability. It is simple to form and run, can be owned by someone who does not live in the United States, and keeps your personal assets separate from your business.

Good to know: an LLC owned by a non-resident generally owes no US federal income tax on its profits if it has no business activity in the United States, but you may still be taxable in your country of residence. Please check with a tax adviser.
